# MildMate Custom Spill-Proof Cushion Protector

## Recommended Product Name

**Custom Spill-Proof Cushion Protector – 360° Elastic TPU Cover for Sofa, Ottoman, RV & Boat Cushions**

---

## Product Positioning

A made-to-measure TPU inner protector designed for sofa cushions, ottomans, bench cushions, RV cushions, boat cushions, window seats, and other specialty cushions.

Unlike a fully enclosed zippered encasement, this fitted-style protector covers the **top and all four sides** while leaving most of the underside open. A continuous **360° elastic band** wraps underneath the cushion edge to help keep the protector securely in place.

The protector is intended to sit **under a decorative outer cushion cover**.

**Recommended layer setup:**

**Cushion / Existing Upholstery → MildMate TPU Protector → Outer Fabric Cover**

## Key Features

### Custom Made to Your Cushion Size
Made individually according to the customer's finished cushion dimensions:

**Width × Length × Thickness**

### Spill-Proof TPU Barrier
TPU fabric helps protect the covered areas from accidental spills, moisture, body oils, pet-related messes, and everyday accidents.

> Note: The TPU fabric provides the liquid barrier. Because the finished product contains stitched seams, the complete sewn protector should be marketed as **spill-proof / water-resistant protection**, rather than guaranteeing that every seam is completely watertight.

### 360° Elastic Grip
Continuous elastic around the entire underside helps hold the protector securely around the cushion.

### Open-Bottom Construction
The top and four sides are covered while most of the underside remains open.

This reduces unnecessary TPU material underneath the cushion and is useful for attached or fixed cushions.

### Approx. 2 in / 5 cm Tuck-Under
The design requires approximately **2 inches / 5 cm of accessible space underneath the cushion edges** so the elastic can wrap underneath and grip securely.

### Flexible and Lightweight
TPU is flexible and suitable for use as a protective inner layer beneath a normal decorative cushion cover.

### Easy Wipe-Off
Small spills can be wiped promptly with a soft damp cloth.

### Made for Specialty Applications
Suitable for non-standard, oversized, deep, RV, marine, sofa, ottoman, bench, and custom furniture cushions.

The 360° elastic version requires approximately:

**2 in / 5 cm of accessible underside space around the cushion edges**

For attached ottomans or fixed cushions, customers should confirm that this space is available before ordering.

## Care Instructions

### Everyday Cleaning
- Wipe spills promptly with a soft damp cloth
- Allow to air dry completely before replacing the outer cushion cover

### Machine Washing
- Machine wash cold
- Use a gentle cycle
- Use mild detergent
- Do not bleach
- Do not use fabric softener
- Air dry whenever possible
- If tumble drying is necessary, use low heat only
- Avoid prolonged high heat

To help extend the life of the TPU layer, use the protector underneath a regular decorative fabric cover.
