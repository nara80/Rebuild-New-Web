## 🌟 10 IN / 25 CM UNDER-TUCK ALLOWANCE

The pattern allowance is calculated using:

> **Confirmed mattress depth + approximately 10 in / 25 cm of under-tuck**

The mattress depth covers the fabric needed to travel down the mattress side. The additional 10 in / 25 cm remains available for tucking underneath.

### Default Calculation

When the actual mattress depth is unavailable, we use:

- Default mattress depth: **12 in / 30 cm**
- Under-tuck allowance: **10 in / 25 cm**
- Total extension beyond the mattress top outline: approximately **22 in / 55 cm**

### Examples

- 8 in mattress depth + 10 in tuck = approximately 18 in total extension
- 10 in mattress depth + 10 in tuck = approximately 20 in total extension
- 12 in mattress depth + 10 in tuck = approximately 22 in total extension

When you provide the actual mattress depth, it replaces the 12 in / 30 cm default.

If the two mattresses have different depths, please provide both measurements. Unless otherwise agreed, we will calculate the allowance using the deeper mattress to provide sufficient tuck coverage.

The visible amount tucked underneath may vary slightly depending on the mattress depth, bed frame and selected tuck edges.

## 🌟 TUCK PREFERENCE

Please tell us where you want the additional tuck allowance:

- Both long sides and foot

If no tuck preference is supplied, we will prepare the sheet with allowance around the outer perimeter of the combined mattress layout.

## 🌟 PLEASE MEASURE BEFORE ORDERING

Standard mattress names are guides only. Dimensions can vary by brand, model and manufacturer.

Please provide the actual measurements of both mattresses:

- Mattress 1: Width × Length × Depth
- Mattress 2: Width × Length × Depth

Please also confirm:

- Measurement unit: inches or centimetres
